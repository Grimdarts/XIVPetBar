# Changelog

All notable changes to XIVPetBar. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-01
### Fixed
- Size, scale, theme and position choices now come back at the next launch. The addon loads
  before login, and the bar was not rebuilt when the character's saved settings arrived.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-31
### Added
- First version: pet HP/MP/TP bar in xivbar's style under xivbar, pet name, job line for SMN,
  BST, PUP, DRG and GEO, `//petbar test` preview, full/compact, scale, theme and position commands.
